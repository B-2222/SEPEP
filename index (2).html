Publish your changes

Nothing goes live until you do this. Takes about fifteen seconds.

  1. Hit Copy the file
  2. Click Open data.js on GitHub, it opens in a new tab
  3. In that tab press Cmd+A then Cmd+V to replace everything
  4. Scroll down, click the green Commit changes
  5. Wait a minute, then refresh the site with Cmd+Shift+R
Open data.js on GitHub

If the site goes blank after a commit, go to the repo, click History, and revert to the last version that worked. Nothing is ever lost.
